Privacy Policy

For the Outlastlab publishing app · Last updated 21 September 2026

What the app does

Outlastlab is a private tool used only by the owner of the Outlast Lab Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account. It publishes the page's own marble race videos as Reels and Stories, and reads the page's own performance statistics. It has no public sign-up and is not offered to other people.

Data we handle

  • Page access token. Stored only on the page owner's computer, so the app can post to the owner's own Page and Instagram account.
  • Page and post statistics. Totals like plays, reach, watch time, likes and comment counts for the page's own posts, kept on the owner's computer to decide which videos work best.
  • No personal data about viewers. The app does not collect, store or process names, messages, profiles or any other personal information of people who view, like or comment on the page's posts.

This website uses no cookies, advertising trackers or analytics.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is only exchanged with Meta Platforms, through its official APIs, to publish the page's content and read its statistics.

Data deletion instructions

The app stores no personal data about you. If you have interacted with Outlast Lab and want related data removed, or want to remove the app's access to your account:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. Find Outlastlab and select Remove. This revokes its access immediately.
  3. To ask us to delete anything else, send a message to the Outlast Lab Facebook Page with the subject “Data deletion”. We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

Contact

Send a message to the Outlast Lab Page on Facebook or Instagram.
